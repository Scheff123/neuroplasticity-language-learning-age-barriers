🔬 Abstract  
Studying foreign languages is a crucial aspect of cognitive development across the human lifespan. However, age-related decline in neuroplasticity presents challenges to effective language acquisition, particularly in adults. This research explores how neuroplasticity—the brain's ability to reorganize neural pathways in response to learning—can be leveraged in modern pedagogical approaches. By tailoring methods to the learner's age and cognitive capacity, it is possible to stimulate cognitive functions, improve pronunciation, and overcome psychological and physiological barriers to learning. The work emphasizes practical strategies like phonetic training with physical tools and integration of multilingual exposure, offering guidance for educators and learners seeking effective, age-conscious language instruction.
